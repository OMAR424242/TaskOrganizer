Delete your One Thing account

This ends your account and removes everything stored with it. You do not need the app installed to do this.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and the email address it uses.
  • Everything held on the server: your tasks, topics, history and settings.
  • Any device still signed in stops syncing.

What this cannot reach

Anything saved on a phone or laptop you still have. One Thing keeps a copy on each device, and that copy is yours. To clear it, open the app on that device and use Reset everything at the bottom of the profile screen.

Do it now

Sign in with the account you want deleted.

Your account and everything stored with it have been deleted.

Nothing further is needed. If you have the app on a phone, the copy there is still yours until you clear it from inside the app.

Accounts are not switched on for this copy of One Thing, which means there is no account to delete — the app was running entirely on your own device.
